About

I'm a machine learning researcher and engineer pursuing an MS in Data Science at the University of Washington, where I focus on building controllable and efficient language-model systems. My interests span LLMs, reinforcement learning, representation learning, and scalable machine learning, with a focus on making intelligent systems more reliable, secure, and efficient.

At the Paul G. Allen School of CSE, I work with Prof. Zaid Harchaoui on controllable language generation, authorship privacy, and autonomous machine learning research. My research addresses the challenge of reducing author-identifying stylistic signals in written text while preserving its original meaning and communicative intent. To tackle this, I am exploring LLM alignment and post-training through feedback-driven evaluation and inference-time control mechanisms to steer model behavior across competing objectives. More recently, I'm looking into building autonomous research agents using reinforcement learning and bandit algorithms with delayed feedbacks to automate ML experimentation and accelerate optimization cycles by 15x.

Prior to UW, I spent over two years as an Applied Scientist at JPMorgan Chase, building and deploying production ML/AI systems for AI Trust & Safety. I've worked across graph learning, multimodal NLP, large-scale entity resolution, fraud detection and distributed ML infrastructure. My work contributed to over $220M in annual fraud savings as well. In summer 2026, I returned to JPMorganChase as an Applied Scientist Intern to work on temporal graph learning for adversarial actor modeling and scalable multi-GPU training.

Earlier, I worked as a MLE at Samsung Research, contributing to production-scale NLU intent routing for the Samsung Bixby assistant and optimizing word-sense disambiguation models through INT8 Quantization-Aware Training. I also conducted research in Human-Computer Interaction and NLP at the Accessible Computing Lab at McGill University as a Mitacs Research Scholar.

I enjoy working at the intersection of research and engineering, taking challenging ML problems from formulation and experimentation to efficient, scalable systems. Open to connect if your team is building something at the intersection of LLM learning, reasoning, and trustworthy AI

Research Interests

My core research interests lie at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, Graph Representation Learning, Human-AI Interaction, Causality, and Trustworthy ML. I focus on designing language and multimodal systems that are interactive, adaptable and explainable to diverse user needs, and capable of operating reliably in high-stakes environments.

  • LLM Post-training, Self-supervision & Evaluation
  • Behavioral AI and Human-Computer Interaction
  • AI Alignment, Privacy-Preserving NLP & Explainability
